# Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

Personal Search Router does not operate a server, collect analytics, sell data, or transmit data to a developer-controlled service.

## What the extension uses

- **Search terms:** The extension reads the search query supplied through Firefox's address bar so it can apply a route such as `g`, `p`, or `o`.
- **Route preferences:** Your custom route settings are stored using Firefox's synchronized extension storage. If Firefox Sync is enabled, Firefox may synchronize those preferences between your Firefox installations.

## Where searches go

Firefox requires the extension's search provider to use an HTTPS URL. Searches therefore first open ChatGPT. Queries beginning with `g`, `p`, or `o` are immediately redirected to Google, Perplexity, or OpenAlex, respectively. As a result, the original query is included in the initial ChatGPT URL even when it is redirected elsewhere.

The privacy practices of ChatGPT, Google, Perplexity, OpenAlex, and Firefox are governed by their respective policies.
